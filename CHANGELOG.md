# Changelog

## 0.9.0
- Separate sliders for lights, blinds and fans (the general slider stays).
- The slider is thicker, keeps room from the tile's edges, and shows the percentage only if asked.
- A light that only switches on and off becomes a toggle on the slider.
- The name under the icon is now a text of your own (it showed the Home Assistant name before).

## 0.8.1
- The slider can be a single cell again, so it can be placed and resized on screens with two columns; it works best on 2 cells or more.

## 0.8.0
- New tile: a slider for dimmable lights, covers and fans. Tap where the bar should reach.

## 0.7.0
- Icon size: large and extra large (the icon drawn scaled).
- Icon by name: any of the 349 icons of Tessera's set; the list has 48 of them (ICONS.md has all names).

## 0.6.0
- New tile: a light button that lists only lights.
- New option: the icon's size, normal or small.

## 0.5.0
- Two more colours: purple and cyan.
- Border position removed again: only the border's colour and width remain.

## 0.4.0
- New tile: a switch button for lights, switches, input_booleans and fans. It toggles the entity and follows its state.
- Border position: outside, medium or inside (rounded).

## 0.3.0
- New options: a coloured border and its width.

## 0.2.0
- Only scripts can be chosen, so the editor's list is short.
- New option: the colour of the icon.

## 0.1.0
- First version: a tile that is only an icon and runs a script when tapped.
